Every month a handful of AI image formats break out of the usual stream and start showing up everywhere. This roundup collects the ones worth your time this month, each with a tested prompt you can copy in one click and the image it produced.

All six work with **Gemini** and **ChatGPT**. Most start from one of your own photos; the product and anime prompts work just as well without one.

## 1. Hugging your younger self (Polaroid)

![An adult hugging his younger self, shot as an instant-film Polaroid](/uploads/polaroid-with-my-younger-self.jpg)

The emotional favourite of the month. Upload a current photo and a childhood photo, and the prompt combines them into a faded instant print of you hugging yourself as a child. The trick is using two real references rather than asking the model to imagine your younger face.

**Prompt:** [Polaroid With My Younger Self](/prompts/polaroid-with-my-younger-self/) · **Guide:** [How to make the younger-self Polaroid](/blog/ai-hug-your-younger-self-polaroid-prompt/)

## 2. Desk collectible figurine with box

![A painted collectible figurine on a desk with its 3D model and retail box](/uploads/desk-collectible-figurine-with-box.jpg)

The figurine trend refuses to die, and this is its most polished form: you as a 1/7 scale painted figure on a clear acrylic base, with the 3D sculpt on the monitor behind and the boxed version beside it.

**Prompt:** [Desk Collectible Figurine With Box](/prompts/desk-collectible-figurine-with-box/) · **Guide:** [AI figurine and action figure prompts](/blog/ai-action-figure-and-figurine-prompts/)

## 3. 90s yearbook portrait

![A 1990s high-school yearbook portrait in a denim jacket on a mottled blue backdrop](/uploads/90s-yearbook-portrait.jpg)

Mottled blue backdrop, soft school-picture lighting, film grain and a slightly awkward smile. This version aims for a real scan rather than a costume party, which is why it gets shared as "is this actually you?"

**Prompt:** [90s Yearbook Portrait](/prompts/90s-yearbook-portrait/) · **Guide:** [The AI 90s yearbook photo prompt](/blog/ai-90s-yearbook-photo-prompt/)

## 4. Neon rain cyberpunk street

![A man in black techwear holding a clear umbrella in a neon-lit rainy alley](/uploads/neon-rain-cyberpunk-street.jpg)

Cyberpunk is everywhere, but most prompts produce the same purple haze. This one uses split lighting — magenta on one side of the face, cyan on the other — and a clear umbrella that catches the rain and the neon right beside your face. Signs use abstract glyphs so you do not get garbled fake words.

**Prompt:** [Neon Rain Cyberpunk Street](/prompts/neon-rain-cyberpunk-street/)

## 5. Hand-painted anime countryside

![A hand-painted anime still of a girl in a white dress beside a rice field under huge summer clouds](/uploads/hand-painted-anime-countryside.jpg)

The warm, hand-painted anime look, described by its ingredients — gouache backgrounds, cel shading, towering cumulus clouds — rather than by naming a studio. Put the character in the lower third of a wide frame and the landscape becomes the subject.

**Prompt:** [Hand-Painted Anime Countryside](/prompts/hand-painted-anime-countryside/) · **More:** [Anime and illustration prompts guide](/blog/anime-and-illustration-prompts/)

## 6. Levitating sneaker splash shot

![A white and teal running shoe floating in a frozen water splash on a teal background](/uploads/levitating-sneaker-splash-shot.jpg)

For small shops and creators: upload a product photo and get an ad-ready hero image with the product floating in a frozen splash. The most important line tells the model *not* to redesign your product.

**Prompt:** [Levitating Sneaker Splash Shot](/prompts/levitating-sneaker-splash-shot/) · **More:** [Product photography prompts guide](/blog/product-photography-ai-prompts/)

## Tips that apply to every trend this month

- **Use a clear, front-facing reference photo.** The face should be large in the frame, well lit and unfiltered. Filters and beauty smoothing carry straight into the result.
- **Run the same prompt in two tools before rewriting it.** Gemini and ChatGPT fail differently; if one drifts, the other often nails it.
- **Name your signature features.** "Keep my round glasses and side parting" survives stylisation far better than "preserve my identity".
- **Watch for fake text and stray logos.** Signs, boxes and borders are where models invent misspelled words or real brand marks. Check before posting.
