The AI yearbook trend keeps coming back because the format is instantly recognisable. Everyone knows the mottled blue backdrop, the slightly awkward smile and the soft studio light from school picture day. The trouble is that most yearbook prompts lean into costume: huge hair, neon windbreakers, a laser background that looks like a screensaver. The result is a parody, not a photo.

This prompt aims for the opposite: a portrait that could genuinely have been scanned from a 1996 yearbook.

## Why yearbook prompts are so reliable

School portraits were one of the most standardised kinds of photography ever made. For about a decade, every school used nearly the same pose, framing, backdrop and lighting. Image models have seen enormous numbers of these photos, so describing the format precisely is enough to land the look. You are describing a standard, not inventing a style.

That gives you four sections to get right.

### 1. Pose and framing

Head and shoulders, body turned slightly to one side, chin level, eyes straight to the lens. Ask for *a warm, slightly self-conscious school-photo smile*. That small awkwardness is a big part of what makes it believable.

### 2. The backdrop

`classic mottled studio backdrop in soft blue-grey with a faint laser-light texture, slightly out of focus`. "Mottled" is the key word; it produces the cloudy painted canvas look. Swap the colour to brown or teal for a slightly earlier or later feel.

### 3. The lighting

A large soft key light from the front, gentle fill, and a subtle hair light behind. The hair light is the detail people do not notice consciously but miss immediately when it is gone: it puts a soft glow around the head and separates you from the backdrop.

### 4. The film

`35mm portrait film, glossy print`, a slightly warm cast, mild grain and *no digital retouching*. Modern phone sharpness and beauty smoothing are the fastest way to make the picture look fake, so the prompt rules them out explicitly.

## Styling without costume

The prompt gives the model a short list of era-appropriate outfits — denim jacket over a plain tee, a knitted sweater vest, a flannel shirt — and asks for hair *styled naturally for the subject*. It usually picks something that suits your face. To control it, replace the list with one exact outfit:

- `a forest-green crewneck sweater over a white collared shirt`
- `an oversized flannel shirt in red and navy, buttoned to the top`
- `a black turtleneck and small silver hoop earrings`

Keep it to clothes people actually wore to picture day. Anything that looks like a fancy-dress shop pushes the result back into parody.

## Make it look scanned, not designed

Many models, if you say "yearbook", design an entire page around the photo: names, a grid of other students, a school crest. That is why the prompt asks for *a real scan from a 1990s yearbook page* and then rules out text, names, layouts and borders. You get one clean portrait, which is far easier to share and much more convincing.

If you *want* the page, generate the portrait first and lay it out yourself, so the model is not also trying to spell twenty names.

## Variations

- **80s yearbook:** brown or grey laser backdrop, `feathered hair`, `wide-collared shirt`, stronger warm cast.
- **2000s yearbook:** plain grey or blue gradient backdrop, `digital camera look, slightly flat colour`, `frosted tips` or `side-swept fringe`.
- **Teacher portrait:** same lighting, older styling, `cardigan and lanyard`. It makes a surprisingly good team page.

## FAQ

**What is the AI yearbook trend?**
Uploading a selfie to an AI image tool and turning it into a retro school yearbook portrait, usually styled after the 1990s.

**Which app is best for AI yearbook photos?**
Gemini and ChatGPT both produce convincing results with the [full prompt](/prompts/90s-yearbook-portrait/). The example above was made in Gemini.

**Why does mine look too smooth or too modern?**
Keep the film section and add `visible film grain, no skin retouching, slight softness`. Also check the reference: heavily filtered selfies carry their smoothing into the result.
