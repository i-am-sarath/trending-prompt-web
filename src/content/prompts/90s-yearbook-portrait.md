Yearbook portraits are one of the most rigid photographic formats ever produced, which is exactly why this prompt is reliable. Pose, framing, backdrop and lighting were essentially identical in every school in the country for a decade, and the model has seen that formula many times. BACKGROUND and LIGHTING are describing a standard, not inventing a look.

STYLING is the clause to edit. The examples are there to steer the model toward the right decade without locking it into one outfit, and it will usually pick something that fits the face in your reference. If you want a specific look, replace the list with one exact outfit.

The last line matters more than it seems. Ask for "a yearbook" and many models will design a whole page with names and layout grids around the portrait. Asking for a scan of a single photo with no text keeps the frame clean and makes the result look found rather than designed.
