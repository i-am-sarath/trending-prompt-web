The clause doing the most work here is **"rain-slicked streets reflecting pink and cyan light"**. Without it you get a city with neon in it; with it you get a city where the neon has somewhere to land. Wet ground doubles every light source in the frame and gives the lower half of the image something to do, which is why almost every memorable cyberpunk still is shot after rain.

The second most useful clause is **"in Japanese and English"**. Models render text poorly, but specifying two scripts pushes toward dense, layered signage rather than a few large legible words — and illegible signage at a distance reads as authentic, where a misspelt English word reads as a mistake. Lean into the failure mode instead of fighting it.

**What to change.** Swap `flying cars` for `a single figure under a translucent umbrella` to shift from establishing shot to character moment. Replace `cinematic wide-angle` with `85mm, shallow depth of field` to compress the neon into bokeh behind a subject.

**What to cut.** `ultra detailed, 8k` are doing nothing measurable on current models — see the [prompt structure guide](/blog/how-to-write-ai-image-prompts) on why quality incantations stopped mattering. The prompt is kept intact here because it is the exact text that produced this image, not the tidied version.
