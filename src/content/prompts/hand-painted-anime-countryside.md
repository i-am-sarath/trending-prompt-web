The hand-painted anime look is everywhere, but naming a specific studio in a prompt invites refusals and dated, copy-paste results. This prompt describes the ingredients instead: gouache and watercolour backgrounds, traditional cel shading, towering cumulus clouds, and a slow slice-of-life setting. You get the same feel, and the result belongs to you rather than imitating someone's frame.

The SKY gets its own section on purpose. In this style the clouds carry half the mood, and when they're folded into the environment line models tend to paint a flat blue gradient. Giving the sky its own lines and adjectives is what produces the large, layered summer clouds people actually share.

COMPOSITION is the second lever. Putting the character in the lower third of a wide frame makes the landscape the subject, which is what separates a film still from an anime avatar. If you want a profile picture, switch to a 4:5 head-and-shoulders crop, and expect the mood to change with it.
