The phrase carrying this image is **"as the only light source"**. Models add fill light by default, which flattens everything and is the main reason generated product shots look synthetic. Stating a single source is what produces the long directional shadows that make the objects sit on the shelf rather than float above it.

**"North-facing window"** is a real photographic convention: north light is soft, cool and consistent through the day, which is why studios are built around it. The model has seen that phrase attached to thousands of images with exactly that quality of light.

**"Unglazed"** matters more than it looks. Glazed ceramic is specular and throws highlights; unglazed is matte and shows form through shading alone. One word decides how the entire surface renders.

**Counting the objects.** "Three vessels" gives a composition. "Some vessels" gives an unpredictable arrangement — and odd numbers group more comfortably than even ones, which is a rule from still-life painting that survives into prompting.

**To push it further.** Add `subtle contact shadow beneath each base` — a missing contact shadow is the most common tell in AI product imagery. The full checklist is in the [product photography guide](/blog/product-photography-ai-prompts).
