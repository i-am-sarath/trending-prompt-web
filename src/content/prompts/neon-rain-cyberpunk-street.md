Cyberpunk is one of the most over-prompted styles there is, and the generic version ("cyberpunk city, neon, 8k") gives you the same purple soup every time. This prompt holds up because of two specific choices.

The first is the split lighting: magenta on one side of the face and cyan on the other. Naming the colours and which side they fall on turns a vague "neon glow" into a real lighting setup, which gives the face shape and keeps it recognisable instead of washing it out in a single tint.

The second is the clear umbrella. It gives the rain something to land on, catches the coloured light right next to the face, and frames the head the way a halo would. Take it out and the rain usually turns into a faint texture over the whole image.

The note about glyphs instead of words is not decoration. Without it, models fill every sign with garbled pseudo-text, and that is the first thing that gives an AI image away.
