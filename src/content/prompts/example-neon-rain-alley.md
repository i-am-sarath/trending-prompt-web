This is the character-scale counterpart to a city-scale establishing shot, and the clause that makes it work is **"stacked six storeys high"**. A number forces vertical composition. Without it the signage spreads horizontally and the alley stops feeling narrow; with it, the frame fills upward and the walls close in.

**"Steam venting from a noodle stall"** is doing two jobs. It is a story detail, and it is atmosphere for the light to pass through — light is invisible until it hits something, so a steam source is what turns neon into visible glow rather than flat colour. The [lighting guide](/blog/lighting-terms-for-ai-prompts) covers this in more detail.

**"One figure"** is a deliberate count. Unspecified people become a crowd, and a crowd removes the loneliness the image depends on.

**Why 35mm.** A wider lens would show too much of the alley and break the enclosure; an 85mm would flatten the depth that makes the signage recede. 35mm with shallow depth of field keeps the viewer inside the space rather than observing it — the distinction explained in [camera and lens terms](/blog/camera-and-lens-terms-for-ai-prompts).

**Variation worth trying.** Add `CineStill 800T` for halation bloom around each sign. It is the single most effective addition to any neon-at-night prompt.
