Fantasy portraits fail in two common ways. Either the armor looks like shiny cosplay plastic, or the helmet covers the face and the identity is gone. This prompt addresses both directly.

"Helmet off, face fully visible" in SUBJECT is non-negotiable if the picture is meant to be you. ARMOR then does the heavy lifting on realism: weathered dark steel, engraved vines, real dents and dirt. Every word pushes away from polished costume metal and toward something that has been worn on a long journey. If you want a cleaner, royal look, swap that clause for gilded ceremonial plate, but expect a less grounded result.

The glowing spores are the only magical element, and that restraint is deliberate. One small fantasy detail in an otherwise physically believable forest reads as cinematic. Stack three or four (glowing runes, floating crystals, magic aura) and the image slides into a game poster. The sword planted point-down also gives the hands a clear, simple job, which avoids most hand distortion.
